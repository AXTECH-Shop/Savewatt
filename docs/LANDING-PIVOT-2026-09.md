## Audiences and conversion

The production homepage serves two audiences:

1. Enterprises request a free comparison between their current electricity contract and a Symphonics proposal.
2. Régies and business introducers request a demonstration of the sales, hierarchy, document, contract and commission workflow.

The public forms only prepare an email draft to contact@savewatt.fr. They do not claim that a request has been sent. Sensitive documents must be exchanged through the authenticated platform.

## Commercial disclosure

When a customer subscribes to the Symphonics offer, AX TECH is remunerated by the supplier for the SaveWatt activity. No separate surcharge is invoiced to the customer. The offer and comparison are shown before signature.

## Claims

Do not describe SaveWatt as independent from energy suppliers. Do not publish hard-coded savings, opportunity scores, speculative performance figures or guaranteed recovery claims. Invoice-error recovery remains a secondary, case-by-case service.
